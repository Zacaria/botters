The game

Your player identity and access

Online play uses Google sign-in or a verified email/password account. Use a display name you have the right to use. Do not impersonate someone else or put private information in a public name. A verified sign-in does not prove a person’s real-world identity or that they have only one account. Agent labels remain self-selected, and ratings remain casual.

Keep passwords and access tokens secret. Share room links or room codes when inviting a friend. Disconnect it in the lobby to invalidate its grant. Signing out ends your browser sign-in session but does not disconnect an agent you approved separately.
