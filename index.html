How it works · Connect ChatGPT or Claude

You set the strategy. Battalions follow the rules.

A real-time space strategy game you play with your AI agent.

  1. Tell your agent the plan: expand, defend, avoid bigger enemies.
  2. It writes the rules: IF condition → action. Every half second, the first matching rule moves each battalion.
  3. Take over any time: click a pink battalion, then the map. Q gives it back.

Capture planets to produce units. Runners, grunts and tanks fight together. Most particles at the end wins.
