How it plays

Every swarm follows your side's gambits: an ordered list of IF condition → action rules. The first rule that matches decides, every half second.

Your agent rewrites them as the match evolves. You can edit them too, switch a rule off, or grab one swarm and send it yourself.

Square law: in a fight, each side loses in proportion to the other's size, so the bigger swarm wins and loses little. Planets you own are eaten into new particles. Fog: you only see near your swarms and planets.

Most particles at the end wins. No swarms and no planets left loses at once.
