You set the strategy. Swarms follow the rules.

A real-time space strategy game you play with your AI agent.

  1. Tell your agent the plan: expand, defend, avoid bigger enemies.
  2. It writes the rules: IF condition → action. Every half second, the first matching rule moves each swarm.
  3. Take over any time: click a pink swarm, then the map. G gives it back.

Capture planets to grow. Bigger swarms win fights. Most particles at the end wins.

Rules, fog and winning

Gambits run top to bottom every half second: the first enabled matching rule whose action can run decides. Manual swarms bypass gambits until released.

Stay on a planet to capture it. Owned planets are consumed into new particles until exhausted.

Only the front of two touching swarms fights, and a swarm much bigger than its enemy wraps it. Half of what a swarm kills joins it, but digesting makes it slow. A swarm that touches no planet and kills nothing for too long starves: watch the red ring.

Colour is the camp. A big swarm that wraps a smaller one bites from all sides: bring more, and bring it together.

Fog hides enemy swarms outside vision near your swarms and owned planets. Planet locations, ownership and both sides' particle totals are public; the enemy total is not a position reveal.

At time, more swarm particles wins; equal totals draw. With no swarms and no owned planets with mass left, a side is wiped out immediately.
